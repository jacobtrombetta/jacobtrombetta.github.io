Greening has been getting together and writting music since 2020. Our self-titled debut album was recorded on Sunday afternoons over a period of three years. The band features members of The Six Parts Seven, talons’, and Annabel.

  • Jay Karpinski - drum kit
  • Scott Moses - bass
  • Mike Tolan - guitar, Rhodes
  • Jacob Trombetta - guitar, pedal steel, Wurlitzer

Get a vinyl copy at Storm Chasers LTD, download it on Bandcamp, or listen on any streaming service.
