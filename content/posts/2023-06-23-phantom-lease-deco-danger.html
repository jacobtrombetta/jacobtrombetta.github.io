Deco Danger is the third album by Phantom Lease. This collection of music was exclusively composed for an arcade game that had a fleeting existence of just one week. This ephemeral gem was showcased at an aluminum can exhibition in 2019. credits

released June 30, 2023

Phantom Lease is Jacob Trombetta and Brenden Vencel
